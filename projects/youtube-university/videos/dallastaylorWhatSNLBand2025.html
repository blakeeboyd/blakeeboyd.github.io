Takeaway

Alex Raytses, filling in for SNL house band monitor mixer Chris Costello, walks Dallas Taylor through the monitor engineering for Saturday Night Live's music. He covers the SSL Live 500 console handling the house band and guest acts simultaneously, the mix of in-ear monitors and wedges, the Dante personal controllers that let some band members shape their own mixes, and the redundancy mix he always builds even when a guest band brings their own monitor engineer so the show goes on if their rig fails.

Before you watch

Prerequisites

  • Familiarity with monitor mixing (in-ears, wedges, personal mixes)
  • Awareness that live TV has multiple band and guest configurations
  • Some exposure to digital mixing consoles

By the end you'll be able to

  • Explain why SNL monitor mixing requires redundancy even when a guest brings their own engineer
  • Describe how Dante personal controllers distribute monitor mixing responsibility between the engineer and the musicians
  • Identify the trade-off between the monitor engineer's personal preferences and the on-air mix
  • Walk through the SSL Live 500 layout Raytses uses (VCAs front, inputs back)

Watch for

  • 0:36 the mixed in-ears/wedges setup and Dante personal controllers
  • 1:47 the redundancy mix philosophy: always have a backup signal path
  • 2:55 the on-air mix priority over personal preference
