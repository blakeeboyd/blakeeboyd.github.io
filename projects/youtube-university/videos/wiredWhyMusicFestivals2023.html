Takeaway

A brief WIRED explainer with Dave Rat (who has provided audio for Coachella since 2001) on how the vertical line array transformed music festivals. The video walks through point-source vs. line-array configurations, the comb-filtering pattern of a point-source field (beamy hot spot in the middle, fades on the sides), the L-Acoustics aiming software that made angled-box line arrays practical to deploy in the field, and a then-and-now photo tour of Coachella that traces the transition from 90 four-foot boxes per side to slim line arrays to the K1 system with 24-box hangs. Rat closes with his view that the goal is to "make the sound system invisible."

Before you watch
