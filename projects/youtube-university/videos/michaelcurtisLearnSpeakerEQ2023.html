Takeaway

A hands-on Open Sound Meter tutorial that lets you practice EQ workflow without a live system. Curtis sets up a transfer-function measurement with a Fostex speaker, creates a math source with the apply function, and attaches three peak filters that act as if they were a hardware EQ on the signal chain. Curtis applies Pat Brown's progressive-narrowing workflow inside the software: capture raw response, view it at 1-point-per-octave smoothing (very coarse), apply broad filters, then narrow to 1/3-octave smoothing and add more surgical filters. You can also invert the captured trace to use as a visual guide for what filter to apply.

  • Configure Open Sound Meter measurement settings (transform mode, sample rate, smoothing)

  • Apply Pat Brown's progressive-narrowing workflow in software
  • Recognize how small EQ moves affect phase response visually
