Takeaway

A 90-second snippet from Lively's 110 Questions series. Michael asks how to make tuning faster when the schedule is tight, and Lively gives three concrete moves: plan and name every microphone position the night before (on-axis A1, off-axis A1, crossover, and so on) along with the action you will take at each one; run multiple measurement microphones in parallel if the gear is available; and push verification work like polarity checks back into the workshop before the gear leaves the shop. Lively names the failure mode he is trying to prevent: taking the right measurement at the wrong position, then either redoing the work or, worse, not catching the mistake until the show has already started.
