MIC LEVEL vs LINE LEVEL: Audio Levels Explained

Takeaway

The video names the four signal levels in professional audio: mic level, instrument level, line level, and speaker level. Each is defined by where it sits and what device raises it to the next. A microphone converts air-pressure changes into very weak electrical currents, so a microphone preamp boosts mic level to line level. A guitar pickup produces a similarly weak signal, and a preamp lifts instrument level to line level. Line level is the interconnect standard: strong enough for consoles, outboard effects, and amplifiers, but not strong enough to drive a speaker. A power amplifier takes line level and outputs speaker level. The video closes by tracing a mic and an electric guitar through their preamps, through effects, and into an amp that delivers speaker level to a loudspeaker.
