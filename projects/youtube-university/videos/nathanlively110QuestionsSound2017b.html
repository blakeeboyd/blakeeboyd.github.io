Takeaway

A short clip in Lively's reader Q&A series, answering Malcolm's question about whether mastery of sound system tuning is reachable. Lively reframes the question by swapping art for science, then partitions the work: a few pass/fail procedures can be learned in minutes and start helping shows right away, while other parts of the craft will take years of practice. He closes by reading the act of asking the question as evidence that the answer is yes, since caring about the shows you are working on is the trait that produces mastery over time.
