How to Play

  1. Start a New Round: Click "New Round" to generate a hidden EQ curve applied to the audio.
  2. Listen to the Target: With "Target EQ" selected, listen to how the audio has been shaped.
  3. Adjust Your EQ: Switch to "Your EQ" and drag the control points on the graph (or use the number inputs below) to recreate what you hear.
  4. Compare: Toggle between "Target EQ" and "Your EQ" to refine your match.
  5. Submit: When confident, click "Submit Guess" to reveal the target curve and see your score.

Tips: Use "Bypass" to hear the unprocessed audio for reference. Scroll over a control point to adjust Q (bandwidth) for peaking bands. Higher scores require closer matches across all frequencies.
